Chapter 04 · From My Heart

A Letter
For You.

My Beautiful Wife,

Happy Birthday to the most beautiful person in my life.

Sometimes I wonder how I got so lucky to have you beside me. You are more than my wife. You are my best friend, my safe place, my happiness, and the person I want beside me through every chapter of life.

You have seen me at my best and at my worst, and somehow you still choose to stand beside me.

Your smile makes ordinary days beautiful. Your voice makes difficult days easier. Your love makes this life feel complete.

I promise that no matter how many years pass, I will keep choosing you. I will keep supporting your dreams. I will keep making memories with you. And I will keep finding new reasons to fall in love with you.

On your birthday, I don't just wish you happiness for today.

I wish you a lifetime of happiness, laughter, adventures, peace and love.

And I hope I get to experience all of it right there beside you.

Forever yours,
Your Husband ❤️
